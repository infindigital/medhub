The idea

Calm like a good clinic. Considered like a design store.

Warm paper tones and deep ink set a calm, editorial frame. Product photos sit on soft sand tiles, so the mixed white-background images look curated rather than catalogue-like. The one lime accent marks the moment to act. The Dubai feel comes from warmth, restraint and a subtle arch silhouette, not from skylines or gold.

Why it can suit MedHub: most catalogue photos are small JPGs on white. Sand tiles with mix-blend-mode: multiply absorb the white and give the grid a consistent look without re-shooting.
