The idea

A medical-tech product company, not a catalogue.

Cool porcelain surfaces, graphite type and a confident ultramarine present MedHub like the device makers it stocks. Bento grids break information into scannable modules. Monospace "spec" labels give a precise, instrument-like tone, and a mint highlight adds the human, Gen-Z note. Dubai shows up through "night" sections (deep navy with an ultramarine glow), which carry the rental and contact moments.

Why it can suit MedHub: it plays to device products (concentrators, CPAP, monitors), and the modular bento pattern suits a catalogue where categories are small (1–25 products each).
