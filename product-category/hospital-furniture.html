How to choose

Choosing a hospital bed

3 or 5 functions

A 3-function bed adjusts the back, legs and height. A 5-function bed adds tilt positions. ICU beds offer the widest range.

Mattress included?

Several beds come with a mattress. Check the product name and description before you order.

Around the bed

Overbed tables, commode chairs, wheelchairs, patient lifters and pressure mattresses complete a care setup.
