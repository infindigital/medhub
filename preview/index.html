How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery within the UAE costs AED 25. Orders are processed within 24 hours of payment confirmation and delivered within 2–3 business days.

Not at the moment. Delivery is available within the United Arab Emirates only.

Can I rent equipment instead of buying it?

Yes. Selected equipment, including oxygen concentrators, CPAP machines, hospital beds and patient monitors, is available on monthly rental. Rental prices are shown per month on each product.

How can I pay?

Orders are paid online by card on a secure payment page at checkout.

No. MedHub supplies medical equipment and supplies, not medicines.
